## Overview - Customer Churn Analysis

This project explores customer churn in a telecom dataset to understand why customers leave. The analysis focuses on cleaning the data, exploring patterns, and using visualizations to highlight key trends that can help improve customer retention.


## Business Problem

Customer churn is a key concern in the telecom industry as it directly impacts revenue. In this project, the goal is to:
Understand what factors are linked to customer churn
Explore how contract type, tenure, and pricing affect customer behavior
Identify groups of customers who are more likely to leave
Provide insights that can help improve retention strategies


## Dataset

Telecom customer dataset with demographic and service-related details
Includes features like tenure, contract type, monthly charges, and churn
Used to explore patterns and relationships related to customer behavior


## Tools & Technologies

Explored how key variables like tenure and monthly charges are distributed
Compared churn across different contract types and services
Used visualizations to better understand customer behavior


## Research Questions

- Which customer groups are more likely to churn?
- How does tenure affect customer retention?
- Does contract type influence churn behavior?
- What is the relationship between monthly charges and churn?

## Key Findings

- Customers with shorter tenure tend to churn more
- Month-to-month contracts show higher churn compared to long-term contracts
- Higher monthly charges are often linked with increased churn
- Customers on long-term plans are more likely to stay
